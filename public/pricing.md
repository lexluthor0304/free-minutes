# Free Minutes Pricing

Last updated: 2026-05-23

## Free

- Price: $0
- Users: No account required
- Browser: Chrome desktop
- Includes: realtime transcription, transcript editing, source labels, local audio recording, screenshots, TXT export, Markdown export, and ZIP export
- Notes: Realtime speech-to-text uses the deployed Cloudflare Worker and Cloudflare Workers AI binding.

## Enterprise

- Price: Not offered
- Status: Free Minutes is currently a lightweight public web app, not an enterprise SaaS product.
